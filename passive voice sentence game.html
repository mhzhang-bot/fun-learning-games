📖 Passive Voice Rule

Structure: Subject + be (is/was/were) + Past Participle (+ by ...)

Examples:

  • The cake is eaten. 🎂
  • The girl was taught by teachers. 👧👩‍🏫
  • The smartphone was stolen. 📱

✨ Remember: Use the correct tense of be and the past participle of the verb.

💡 You can use is/are for present or was/were for past.
